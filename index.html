Location

The best of Umhlanga on your doorstep

Umhlanga Arch is an iconic mixed-use development in Ridgeside, Umhlanga Ridge: a secure precinct with a rooftop bar, The Baron restaurant, high-street shopping, a cobbled fountain square and a Hilton Garden Inn. Below you, the coast stretches from Umhlanga’s famous lighthouse to the Durban skyline, with beaches, shopping and some of KwaZulu-Natal’s best restaurants just a short drive away.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where exactly is The Arch?

Both apartments are in Umhlanga Arch, 1 Ncondo Place, Ridgeside, Umhlanga, KwaZulu-Natal. Full arrival instructions are sent with your booking confirmation.

Can we book both apartments together?

Yes. The two apartments are neighbours in the same building, which makes them ideal for families and groups who want to be together but still have their own space. Simply book each apartment for the same dates.

Which apartment should I choose?

The Sea View Apartment has two bedrooms (a king and a twin) and two bathrooms, so it’s perfect for families or two couples. The Luxury Balcony Apartment is a one-bedroom showpiece with 180-degree ocean views, an island kitchen and a freestanding bath, so it’s ideal for couples or a special trip.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Your exact check-in and check-out times are confirmed when you book. If you need an early arrival or late departure, just ask and we’ll do our best to help.

Is there parking?

The Sea View Apartment comes with up to two parking bays. For the Luxury Balcony Apartment, let us know when you book if you’re arriving by car and we’ll send you the parking details.

Is the building secure?

Yes. Umhlanga Arch has biometric access control, 24-hour CCTV and 24-hour security on the ground floor, plus a professional concierge service.

Is the apartment cleaned during my stay?

As holiday apartments we don’t offer daily cleans, but if you’d like your apartment cleaned during your stay, just speak to the concierge and arrange payment through them.

Is it suitable for working remotely?

Yes. Both apartments have Wi-Fi and a dedicated desk, so it’s easy to mix work with time by the sea.

Why book direct rather than through Airbnb?

Booking direct means no platform service fees, so you get our best available rate, and you deal with us personally from enquiry to check-out.
