Location

The best of Umhlanga on your doorstep

Umhlanga Arch is an iconic mixed-use development in Ridgeside, Umhlanga Ridge. On the property you’ll find The Baron and Prodigal Son restaurants, high-end shops, an aesthetic clinic and a Hilton Garden Inn. Below you, the coast stretches from Umhlanga’s famous lighthouse to the Durban skyline, with beaches, shopping and some of KwaZulu-Natal’s best restaurants just a short drive away.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where exactly is The Arch?

Both apartments are in Umhlanga Arch, 1 Ncondo Place, Ridgeside, Umhlanga, KwaZulu-Natal. Full arrival instructions are sent with your booking confirmation.

Can we book both apartments together?

Yes. Both apartments are in the same building, which makes them ideal for families and groups who want to be together but still have their own space. Simply book each apartment for the same dates.

Which apartment should I choose?

The 2 Bedroom Luxury Sea View Apartment has a king room and a twin room and sleeps up to four, so it’s best for families and groups of friends. The Dream View Apartment is a one-bedroom apartment with a penthouse feel and 180-degree ocean views, ideal for couples or a special trip.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is between 15:00 and 21:00, and check-out is before 10:00.

Is there parking?

The Luxury Sea View Apartment comes with up to two parking bays. For the Dream View Apartment, let us know when you book if you’re arriving by car and we’ll send you the parking details.

Is the building secure?

Yes. Umhlanga Arch has biometric access control, 24-hour CCTV and 24-hour security on the ground floor, plus a professional concierge service.

Is the apartment cleaned during my stay?

As holiday apartments we don’t offer daily cleans, but if you’d like your apartment cleaned during your stay, just speak to the concierge and arrange payment through them.

Why book direct rather than through Airbnb?

Booking direct means no platform service fees, so you get our best available rate, and you deal with us personally from enquiry to check-out.
