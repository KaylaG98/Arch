Things to do in Umhlanga

Our favourite ways to spend your days while staying at The Arch. Tap any place to find out more.

Our favourites

Umhlanga Promenade & Lighthouseapprox. 10 min drive Read more

Walk or jog the paved promenade that runs along the beachfront past the famous red-and-white Umhlanga Lighthouse. It’s at its best at sunrise, when the sun comes up straight out of the ocean, and the promenade leads you past cafés, rock pools and the pier.

Umhlanga Main Beachapprox. 10 min drive Read more

Golden sand and warm water most of the year, with lifeguards on duty and shark nets protecting the main bathing areas. Explore the rock pools at low tide, and always swim between the flags.

Umhlanga Lagoon Nature Reserveapprox. 10 min drive Read more

A shady boardwalk winds through coastal dune forest to the lagoon mouth and a quiet stretch of beach. Look out for birds, monkeys and the odd duiker along the way.

Chartwell Driveapprox. 10 min drive Read more

The heart of Umhlanga village and home to some of the best restaurants and nightlife on the coast, from relaxed breakfast spots and coffee shops to cocktail bars and fine dining.

High tea or a meal at The Oyster Boxapprox. 10 min drive Read more

This landmark hotel overlooking the lighthouse is famous for its afternoon high tea and its curry buffet, a true Durban institution. Book ahead for a table with a sea view.

On the propertyAt Umhlanga Arch Read more

Dress up for a night out at The Baron or enjoy a meal at The Prodigal, browse the high-end shops, or book a treatment at the aesthetic clinic, all without leaving Umhlanga Arch.

More to explore

Gateway Theatre of Shoppingapprox. 5 min driveRead more

One of the biggest shopping centres in the southern hemisphere, with hundreds of shops, cinemas and restaurants.

King Shaka International Airportapprox. 15 min driveRead more

The Arch is an easy 15-minute drive from the airport, so you can be on your balcony soon after you land.

KZN Sharks Boardapprox. 10 min driveRead more

Find out about sharks and the region’s shark-safety work with presentations and, on selected mornings, boat trips to see the nets. Check their website for times.

Sibaya Casino & Entertainment Kingdomapprox. 15 min driveRead more

Restaurants, shows and a casino just up the coast.

Moses Mabhida Stadium & the Golden Mileapprox. 20 min driveRead more

Ride the SkyCar to the top of the stadium arch for 360° views of Durban, then walk or cycle along the beachfront promenade.

uShaka Marine Worldapprox. 25 min driveRead more

Africa’s largest aquarium, with a water park, marine shows and a shipwreck to explore. A guaranteed hit with children.

Ballito & the Dolphin Coastapprox. 25 min driveRead more

Quieter beaches, rock pools and seaside restaurants up the North Coast, with dolphins often spotted offshore.

Valley of a Thousand Hillsapprox. 50 min driveRead more

Rolling hills, craft markets, Zulu cultural experiences and country restaurants inland from Durban.

Big Five safari at Hluhluwe-iMfoloziapprox. 3 hr driveRead more

Africa’s oldest proclaimed game reserve and home of the Big Five. Go for a long weekend, or join a guided day tour from Umhlanga.
