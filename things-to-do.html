Things to do in Umhlanga

Our favourite ways to spend your days while staying at The Arch. Tap any place to find out more.

Our favourites

Umhlanga Promenade & Lighthouseapprox. 10 min drive Read more

Walk or jog the paved promenade that runs along the beachfront past the famous red-and-white Umhlanga Lighthouse. It’s at its best at sunrise, when the sun comes up straight out of the ocean, and the promenade leads you past cafés, rock pools and the pier.

Umhlanga Main Beachapprox. 10 min drive Read more

Golden sand and warm water most of the year, with lifeguards on duty and shark nets protecting the main bathing areas. Explore the rock pools at low tide, and always swim between the flags.

Umhlanga Lagoon Nature Reserveapprox. 10 min drive Read more

A shady boardwalk winds through coastal dune forest to the lagoon mouth and a quiet stretch of beach. Look out for birds, monkeys and the odd duiker along the way.

Chartwell Driveapprox. 10 min drive Read more

The heart of Umhlanga village and home to some of the best restaurants and nightlife on the coast, from relaxed breakfast spots and coffee shops to cocktail bars and fine dining.

High tea or a meal at The Oyster Boxapprox. 10 min drive Read more

This landmark hotel overlooking the lighthouse is famous for its afternoon high tea and its curry buffet, a true Durban institution. Book ahead for a table with a sea view.

On the propertyAt Umhlanga Arch Read more

Dress up for a night out at The Baron or enjoy a meal at Prodigal Son, browse the high-end shops, or book a treatment at the aesthetic clinic, all without leaving Umhlanga Arch.
