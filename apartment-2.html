About this apartment

Welcome to our beautiful apartment in the prestigious Umhlanga Arch, known for its picturesque views and prime location. Enjoy stunning 180-degree views of the Indian Ocean from every angle of the living area, and wake up to breathtaking sea views from the bedroom. Experience the best of coastal living in this luxurious space.

Inside, deep charcoal walls, smoked oak joinery, brass lighting and bold contemporary art set the tone. The open-plan living area centres on a sumptuous modular sofa and a timber-slatted media wall with a smart TV and soundbar, leading to a dining table beneath a woven pendant. The kitchen is made for cooking, with a dramatic stone island, double ovens and a large fridge.

Outside, the wide balcony is a second living room, with lounge seating, a round table and a bar overlooking the coastline, perfect for long breakfasts and sunset drinks. The bedroom has a reading nook with velvet armchairs and a desk, plus a spa-like en-suite with a double vanity, emerald-tiled walk-in shower and freestanding bath. A jungle-wallpapered guest cloakroom completes the picture.

The apartment is fully air-conditioned with Wi-Fi throughout.

It’s right next door to the Sea View Apartment, so larger groups can book both and stay together.
